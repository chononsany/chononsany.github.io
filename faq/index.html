FAQ

Frequently asked questions

I've never taken ayahuasca. Can I join?

Yes. Most guests come for the first time. The consultation, preparatory practices and small group exist for exactly this reason.

I take antidepressants or other medication. Can I come?

Tell us during the consultation. Some medications, including SSRIs and MAOIs, are incompatible with ayahuasca and must be tapered with your own doctor weeks in advance. It is decided case by case, and sometimes the answer is no.

Who leads the ceremonies?

Maestro Chonon Sany leads every ceremony himself. He is a medical doctor by training and has practiced Shipibo plant medicine since 2013.

What language are ceremonies and circles in?

The maestro speaks Spanish and Russian. For English speakers we arrange translation; mention it in your application.

How do I get to Tarapoto?

Fly to Lima, then take a direct flight to Tarapoto (TPP), about 1 h 15 min. We pick you up at the airport and drop you off at the end of the retreat.

What diet should I follow before the retreat?

For 1–2 weeks: no alcohol, pork, spicy food, excess salt or sugar, and no recreational drugs. You receive a detailed guide once your place is confirmed.

Can I come as a couple?

Yes, double rooms are available at the same price per person.

How do I pay and what if my plans change?

A US$500 deposit secures your place (bank transfer or PayPal; in Peru also Yape/Plin). If screening advises against participation, the deposit is refunded in full. If you cancel, it transfers to any S.A.N.Y. retreat within 12 months.

Is ayahuasca legal in Peru?

Yes. Ayahuasca has been recognized as National Cultural Heritage of Peru since 2008 and traditional ceremonies are legal. Check the rules of your own country for travel.

Is this a medical treatment?

No. The retreat is a traditional practice, not medical treatment, and it does not replace care from a doctor or therapist. We make no promise to cure any condition.

Ready to talk?

Applying commits you to nothing. The maestro or his team replies personally within 24 hours.
