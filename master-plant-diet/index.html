What a master plant diet is

In the Shipibo tradition, a diet (dieta) is a period of living with a teacher plant: taking it in a prescribed way while keeping to a simple diet and a set of behavioural restrictions. The maestro chooses the plant for the person and accompanies the process.

The diet is quiet, inward work. Many people use it to find inner steadiness, clarity about their questions and a new relationship with themselves. Following the diet and the recommendations is most of the work; the maestro shows the path, and you walk it.

About the plant

The maestro prescribes the plant individually and explains how to obtain it and prepare it where you live; the participant sources the plant themselves. Check the rules on the plant in your country before starting.

Safety

Your place is confirmed after the diagnostic session. Tell the maestro about any medication and health condition: some plants are not combined with certain medicines. The diet is a traditional practice, not medical treatment, and does not replace care from a doctor or therapist.

Frequently asked questions

What does the program include?

A diagnostic session, a preparation plan, an individual diet with a teacher plant, six Zoom sessions (individual or group), daily check-ins in chat, and integration at the end.

Can I ask questions during the diet?

Yes. You report daily in the chat and the maestro answers questions along the way.

Can I continue after 14 days?

Yes, by arrangement with the maestro after the integration session.

Can I do a longer apprentice diet in Peru?

Yes. Longer diets in the jungle are arranged individually; write to discuss dates.

How do I pay?

After the diagnostic session the maestro or his team sends payment details privately.

Ready to talk?

Applying commits you to nothing. The maestro or his team replies personally within 24 hours.
