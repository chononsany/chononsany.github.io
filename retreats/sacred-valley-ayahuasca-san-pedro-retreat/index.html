The journey

Held at Raíces Inkas in the Sacred Valley of the Incas, this retreat combines traditional Shipibo ayahuasca ceremonies led by Maestro Chonon Sany with San Pedro (huachuma) ceremonies and Andean offerings to Pachamama.

Maestro Chonon Sany is joined by Andean ceremonial guides, including Lorenzo Vásquez Pauqkar, a Q'eros Pampa Misayoq, and Chawa Sayri Tupac.

Who it is for

For people who want time: to prepare, to go deep and to integrate before returning home. The small group, comfortable accommodation and the same medical screening as all S.A.N.Y. retreats apply.

The retreat is a traditional practice, not medical treatment, and it does not replace care from a doctor or therapist. We make no promise to cure any condition.
