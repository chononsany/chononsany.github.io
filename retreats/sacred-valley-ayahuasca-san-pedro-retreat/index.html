The journey

Over 13 days you move through a complete arc: arrival, purification, deep ceremony, cultural immersion and conscious return. Nothing is rushed; rest, ceremony, culture and integration support one another.

Raíces Inkas is a luxury sanctuary at the foot of Apu Pitusiray, a mountain revered by the Andean people for thousands of years. Open shalas overlook the peaks, organic meals are prepared fresh each day, and every room is quiet and comfortable.

The ceremonies

  • Four ayahuasca ceremonies with Maestro Chonon Sany, held with Shipibo icaros and personal attention, each building on the last.
  • Two San Pedro (huachuma) ceremonies at sacred outdoor sites, the Temple of the Moon and the ancient water sanctuary of Urco, guided by Chawa Sayri Tupac.
  • Rapé ceremonies, Q'eros rituals and coca leaf reading with Pampa Misayoq Lorenzo Vásquez Pauqkar.

Beyond the maloca

You stand in Qorikancha, the Temple of the Sun in Cusco, walk the circular terraces of Moray and see dawn over Machu Picchu, arriving by first-class train the evening before. Every ceremony is followed by an integration circle, with private time with each guide. On the last day you write a letter to your future self, sent back to you months later, and the retreat closes with a gala dinner cooked with a Peruvian chef and live Andean music.

Your guides

Maestro Chonon Sany

Medical doctor by training (neurosurgery residency, Burdenko Institute, Moscow), Shipibo curandero with the title Onaya Itan Meraya, recognized by the CoshiCox community, and Pampa Misayoq of the Q'eros lineage. Leads the ayahuasca ceremonies.

César Rivas Palacios · Chawa Sayri Tupac

Keeper of the Chavín healing tradition, one of the oldest in the Americas. Guides the San Pedro (huachuma) ceremonies.

Lorenzo Vásquez Pauqkar

Born and initiated in the Q'eros village at 4,200 m. Pampamisayoq who leads offerings to Pachamama and the Apus, coca leaf readings and Q'eros initiations.

Getting there

Fly to Cusco (CUZ); direct flights from Lima take about an hour. Our team meets you and drives you to Raíces Inkas, about 1.5 hours away. The sanctuary sits at about 2,800 m; nights are cool (5–10 °C), so bring warm layers. We recommend arriving 1–2 days early to acclimatize.
